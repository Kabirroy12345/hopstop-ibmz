Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions

=== 2_What_is_the_Problem_you_are_trying_to_solve [670 chars / 96 words] ===
When a cyber-fraud scam succeeds, stolen money is routed through money-mule accounts across multiple banks within minutes over instant payment rails (IMPS/UPI/ACH/Wire). Traditional bank AML transaction monitoring runs in hourly or overnight batches. By the time a batch alert fires—or a victim calls the 1930 Cybercrime Helpline and police issue a freeze request—the funds have already jumped 3 to 5 hops across dozens of accounts and been cashed out. Static velocity rules also suffer from high false-positive rates on legitimate corporate payroll fan-outs and merchant settlements, overwhelming fraud analysts while missing rapid multi-hop pass-through mule layering.

=== 3_How_are_you_planning_to_solve_the_problem [968 chars / 131 words] ===
HopStop embeds real-time money-mule graph scoring into the pre-settlement payment path:
1. Stream & Causal O(1) Feature Engine (hopstop/feature_engine.py): Replays time-ordered IBM AMLworld transfers, hashes account IDs with salted SHA-256, and updates per-account 15m/1h/24h deques (past events only) to extract 12 features: fan-in/out, 15m/1h pass-through ratio, time-since-last-credit, rapid drain flag, counterparty novelty, Welford amount Z-score, and rail risk.
2. Snap ML / Tensor-Tree GBDT Scorer (hopstop/ibmz_runtime.py): Scores each transfer in 0.04 ms (p50) / 0.20 ms (p99) using a 36-tree gradient-boosted ensemble (Snap ML tree_format=auto / compiled tensor-trees).
3. Policy & Carbon Console (hopstop/policy_engine.py, server.py): Calibrates ALLOW / STEP_UP / HOLD thresholds to a 1% alert budget, outputs Top-3 reason codes and SHA-256 hash-chained audit logs, stopping 94.0% of test laundering chains by Hop 2 (PR-AUC 0.8317 vs 0.5306 rules baseline).

=== 4_How_does_your_solution_leverage_IBM_Z_LinuxONE [952 chars / 135 words] ===
1. Co-Located In-Transaction Scoring: Core banking and payment switches run on IBM Z. Running HopStop on the same IBM Z / LinuxONE (s390x) platform eliminates the 5-30 ms network/TLS hop to external cloud AI servers and keeps sensitive transaction graphs on-platform.
2. IBM Telum / Telum II NNPA & Snap ML: hopstop/ibmz_runtime.py probes s390x Facility 165 and libzdnn.so via zdnn_is_nnpa_installed() and configures IBM Snap ML with tree_format='auto' (targeting the Telum Integrated Accelerator for AI via tensor-trees, with automatic fallback to branch-minimized CPU tensor-trees when a guest VM does not expose physical NNPA), achieving 0.202 ms p99 latency and 109,612 TPS (micro-batch=64).
3. Pervasive Encryption & Secure Execution Readiness: Salted SHA-256 account hashing and hash-chained audit logs leverage CPACF hardware crypto on s390x, packaged in Dockerfile.s390x for LinuxONE and IBM Secure Execution (Facility 158) confidential guests.

=== 5_What_value_does_your_solution_deliver [975 chars / 140 words] ===
1. Fraud Operations Analysts: Receive a live sub-millisecond queue of HELD and STEP_UP transfers with Top-3 human-readable reason codes instead of next-day batch alerts.
2. Cybercrime Investigators (1930 Helpline) & Victims: Because HopStop freezes 94.0% of test laundering chains by Hop 1 or Hop 2, stolen victim funds remain in the initial mule account ready to be frozen and recovered before Hop 3+ cash-out.
3. Bank Risk Heads: Enforce a strict analyst alert budget (e.g., 1.0% alert rate) while achieving 53.3% laundering recall (+19.8% relative lift over static velocity rules at 44.5%) and 0.8317 PR-AUC (+56.7% lift over rules at 0.5306).
4. AML Compliance Officers (FIU-IND): Get tamper-evident, time-stamped SHA-256 hash-chained audit records and feature attributions for Suspicious Transaction Reports.
5. Retail Customers: Protected from scam losses while borderline transfers trigger frictionless STEP_UP verification instead of wrongful blanket account freezes.
